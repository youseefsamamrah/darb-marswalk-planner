> ⚠️ **Verify the official length and format limits on 13 Nov 2026** (Project Submission Guide).
> In 2025 the demo was a link to a **30-second video** or a **slide deck of up to 7 slides**. Re-check before recording.

## Privacy rules (from the Space Apps FAQ)
Participants under 18 must **not** use photos, video, voices or likenesses of themselves or others under 18.
So: **screen recording only**. No faces, no names, no webcam. For the voice use **text captions**, a
**text-to-speech voice**, or an **adult** (mentor or parent) reading the script.
