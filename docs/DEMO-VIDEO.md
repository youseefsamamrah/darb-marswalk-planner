# Darb — demo video and slides plan

> ⚠️ **Verify the official length and format limits on 13 Nov 2026** (Project Submission Guide).
> In 2025 the demo was a link to a **30-second video** or a **slide deck of up to 7 slides**. Re-check before recording.

## Privacy rules (from the Space Apps FAQ)
Participants under 18 must **not** use photos, video, voices or likenesses of themselves or others under 18.
So: **screen recording only**. No faces, no names, no webcam. For the voice use **text captions**, a
**text-to-speech voice**, or an **adult** (mentor or parent) reading the script.

---

## Option A — 30-second video

Record the browser at 1920×1080 (Windows: Win + G Game Bar, or OBS). Use the app's **▶ Tour** button (→ key for each
step) or the **Demo** button so every take is the same. The tour captions can stay on screen as the video's text.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over / caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Map of Mount Sharp loads, Curiosity's path visible | "On Mars, a message to Earth takes up to 24 minutes. Darb plans your walk right here." |
| 4–9 s | Toggle Slope → Thermal → Minerals | "Real NASA data: slope from 1-metre HiRISE elevation, ground hardness from THEMIS, minerals from CRISM." |
| 9–15 s | Click **Demo**: dotted route + yellow route card | "A-star finds the safest path: 2.7 km, 61% oxygen left after the return — Caution, some slopes over 10°." |
| 15–19 s | Click the route → real Curiosity Navcam photo | "See what you'll find, in real rover photos." |
| 19–24 s | **Simulate walk** in HUD mode | "Helmet-visor HUD: compass, oxygen timer, warnings." |
| 24–30 s | Ask Darb answers "Is this route safe?" with timing shown | "Ask Darb is an AI that runs offline, on your own device. Darb — the path." |

Captions: white text on a dark bar at the bottom, large font. End frame: "Darb · NASA Space Apps 2026 · data: NASA".

## Option B — 7 slides

1. **Title** — "Darb (دَرْب) — Marswalk Planner". Subtitle: Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map. Map screenshot.
2. **Problem** — 4–24 min light delay; Mars data is scattered; existing tools are regional, not walking-scale.
3. **Our place and goal** — Mount Sharp, Gale Crater; boxwork ridges Curiosity studied in 2025; collect samples.
4. **Data layers** — screenshot with slope / THEMIS / CRISM; small table of the NASA sources.
5. **Safe route** — screenshot of route + card: 2.73 km, 1 h 38 min, 61 % O₂, max slope 10.5°, 🟡 Caution;
   "A* in ~3 ms on 288,000 cells of 25 m (HiRISE 1 m elevation, within 4.2 m of Curiosity's own altitude)".
6. **HUD + buddy mode + Ask Darb** — HUD screenshot; buddy mode at 100 m: 5.29 km detour, 🔴 Danger (two astronauts
   can't fit through the narrow gaps); Ask Darb answer with response time; "runs offline".
7. **Honest limits and next steps** — obstacles smaller than 25 m (boulders, 1–2 m boxwork ridges) are not in the slope;
   next: roughness from the 1 m data and rock detection in HiRISE images; links to repo and SOURCES.md.
