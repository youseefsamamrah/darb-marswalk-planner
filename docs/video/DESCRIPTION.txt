Darb — Marswalk Planner | Team Darb · Ramallah | NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Challenge: Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map.

On Mars, Earth is 4–24 light-minutes away, so an explorer cannot wait for mission control before every step. Darb ("path" in Arabic) is our working prototype that brings the information about the next step into one place: a walking-scale map of lower Mount Sharp, Gale Crater, with terrain slope and rough ground, a thermal proxy for loose sand, mineral signals, a route planner that reports distance, walking time and oxygen reserve, real Curiosity photos of the destination, a helmet-display walk simulation, and "Ask Darb", an AI assistant that runs on the device with no internet.

What you see in this video
Every app screen, route, number, photo and Ask Darb answer is genuine screen footage of the running prototype. The demo route shown is 2.73 km, 1 h 38 min one way, 61% oxygen reserve after the return, rated "Caution". The Ask Darb answer was generated by a local open model (qwen3.5:9b, via Ollama) while the screen was being recorded, at its real speed.

Prototype only
Darb is a student prototype for a hackathon. It is not certified navigation or life-support equipment. Slope limits, walking speed and the 8-hour oxygen budget are our own assumptions. CRISM signals are relative and are not confirmed mineral identifications.
